PerkPop is a lightweight Chrome extension that detects supported online merchants in your Google Search results and displays a small cashback offer banner directly inline — so you see the savings opportunity before you even click through.

How it works:
• Open Google Search and search for anything you might want to buy.
• If a supported merchant appears in the results, PerkPop injects a subtle cashback badge right next to that result.
• No clicks, no popups, no redirects — the offer is just there.

Privacy-first by design:
• No account required. No sign-in.
• No data collection of any kind.
• Your search queries are never read, stored, or transmitted.
• The merchant registry is bundled locally with the extension.
• Chrome storage is used only to cache offer labels on your device for one hour.

Permissions used:
• storage — to cache offer data locally on your device.
• google.com — to read merchant domain names from search result links and inject the cashback badge.

This is an open-source portfolio project demonstrating Manifest V3 extension architecture, Shadow DOM UI injection, and configuration-driven merchant detection.
